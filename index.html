About

I am an IT Consultant with over 4 years of experience delivering enterprise infrastructure projects — in monitoring, IAM and automation — for clients including Volvo Group, Telefónica and the Spanish Ministry of Defence. I hold a BSc in Management Information Systems from Instituto Politécnico de Setúbal.

My work has focused on identity federation (SAML, OIDC, OAuth), access control and systems automation. At Volvo Group I migrated more than 50 enterprise applications from CA SiteMinder to Ping Identity with zero customer complaints. At Telefónica I built a Bash automation suite that reduced weeks of manual BI analysis to a 10-minute run.

I am now channelling this infrastructure background into cybersecurity — certified through the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate and preparing CompTIA Security+ — with a focus on SOC, security operations and IAM engineering roles.

In parallel, I build personal tools — like Job Radar — to learn by doing and make my own job search more effective.
